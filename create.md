# Let us help you build

Whether you're looking to share research or creative projects with a wide audience, publicize your campus office's services to potential users, complete a digital project for a class, or just establish a digital presence with an online portfolio, we can help you get a website up and running to accomplish the task. 

Because our goal is to [empower]({{ site.url }}#how-we-want-to-work-with-you), we won't design, build, or maintain the site *for* you, but we'll help you choose the right tools to realize your vision and provide support as you do the work. 

Anyone with a Geneseo account can create a personal or collaborative WordPress site on [Geneseo OpenLab](https://geneseo.sunycreate.cloud). If your project requires a different kind of site — say an [Omeka](https://omeka.org) site for building exhibits with digitized archival materials, or a [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.org)-like site for building a collaborative knowledge resource — we can help you set up shared hosting on [SUNY Create](https://sunycreate.cloud). There are other options for building and hosting sites: static-site generators like [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com) or [Zola](https://www.getzola.org/), free hosting from [GitHub](https://github.com) or other providers, and commercial hosting plans.

We can help you with open-source tools to enhance your website. [TimelineJS](https://timeline.knightlab.com/) and [StoryMapJS](https://storymap.knightlab.com/) are just two of many.

If you need help with a tool we don't know about or have limited experience with, ask us about it! We'll look into it. We're always eager to expand our own toolkit.
